Topics

What Alex speaks about

Each talk can be pitched at practitioners, leaders, or a mixed room.

Apache Iceberg and open lakehouses

Table formats, catalogs, interoperability, and the practical choices behind a production lakehouse. Alex co-wrote Apache Iceberg: The Definitive Guide and wrote Architecting an Apache Iceberg Lakehouse.

Catalogs and governance

What a lakehouse catalog does, how Apache Polaris and the other options compare, and how to keep one set of tables usable across engines. Alex co-wrote Apache Polaris: The Definitive Guide.

Agentic AI and analytics

Why AI agents need governed, well-described data, and how semantic layers and open interfaces make agent answers dependable.

Performance and optimization

Partitioning, sorting, compaction, row group sizing, and query acceleration, with the tradeoffs of each.

AI-assisted software development

Context engineering, AI-TDD, and harness design for teams using coding agents, the subject of Building the Software Factory (Packt).

Open source and developer communities

How community-run projects become standards, and how to teach complex technology without losing the audience.

For organizers

Organizer FAQ

What topics does Alex speak on?

Apache Iceberg and open lakehouse architecture, lakehouse catalogs such as Apache Polaris, query performance and data optimization, semantic layers, agentic analytics, and how open-source communities form standards. Talks can be pitched at engineers, data leaders, or a mixed audience.

Which formats work?

Keynotes, conference sessions, workshops, panels, meetups, podcasts, and webinars, in person or virtual.

How do fees work?

Put your budget in the inquiry, including if there is none. Community meetups and nonprofit events are welcome to ask.

What about travel?

Say whether travel and lodging are covered and where the event is. Alex is based in Orlando, Florida. A virtual session removes the question entirely.

What should I tell you about AV and recording?

Share the room setup (screen connectors, microphone, confidence monitor), the session length, and whether the talk will be recorded or streamed.

How far ahead should I ask?

As early as you can. Conference calls for papers often close months ahead, so include the date and any CFP deadline in the form.
